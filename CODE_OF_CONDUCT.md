# Code of Conduct

PEPTA Open Science is committed to a respectful, rigorous, and transparent research community.

## Expected behavior

- Use evidence-based language.
- Be respectful in technical disagreement.
- Document assumptions and limitations clearly.
- Protect private, restricted, or sensitive research material.
- Prioritize safety and legal compliance in public contributions.

## Unacceptable behavior

- Harassment or personal attacks.
- Publishing secrets or restricted datasets.
- Misrepresenting research outputs as clinical guidance.
- Posting unsafe biological design instructions.

## Enforcement

Repository maintainers may remove content or restrict participation when behavior or contributions violate these standards.
